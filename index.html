The idea

One trip. Every Adobe tool. Raw file to finished frame.

Justin has run his entire workflow on Adobe for thirteen years, from the phone on the mountain to the laptop in the hotel to the finished Reel. This series shows that workflow in order, on location, so every episode sells the place, the process and a different part of the Creative Cloud.

01 · On the mountain

Lightroom mobile

The raw file on the iPhone at the top of the lift. Scene enhance lifts the snow, a sky mask brings the peak back, the first frame is posted before the lifts close.

02 · At the laptop

Lightroom and Photoshop

The same shoot that night: masking, colour grading and Justin's own presets in Lightroom, then Photoshop for the retouch, the composite and the print-ready master.

03 · The cut

Premiere Pro and After Effects

The episode itself is edited in Premiere Pro, with titles and the before-and-after reveal built in After Effects. The tools are on screen, not just in the credits.

04 · The reach

Reels, Shorts, YouTube

Short-form for the hook, long-form for the full breakdown, a before-and-after carousel for saves. Every post links to Lightroom or Creative Cloud.

Where the episodes get made.

Japan is the launch. New Zealand, the Rockies and Colorado follow before January, and the 2027 calendar runs through Central America to Iceland. One confirmed episode can grow into a year without adding travel.

Japan · the launch episodes

Tokyo, Sapporo, Niseko

20 to 30 November · four days of powder in Niseko from the 27th

  • Episode 1, Niseko powder: Scene enhance on blown-out snow, masked sky, Mount Yotei behind
  • Episode 2, Sapporo at night: a whiteout in neon, white balance and colour grading on the phone
  • Episode 3, Tokyo: Shinjuku rain and reflections, the city edit before the mountain edit

Travel confirmed. Justin is in Japan for these dates regardless, so the launch episodes carry no travel cost and can be live before mid-December.

The series

Three ways to run it.

From a single Lightroom mobile episode in Niseko to a year of the Creative Cloud on the road. Rates and a deliverables outline follow once the shape is agreed.

Option A · Lightroom on the mountain

  • 1 Reel: Scene enhance in Niseko powder, raw file to finished frame on the phone
  • Cross-posted to Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts
  • Before-and-after carousel post with the finished frame
  • Lightroom link in the caption, DM keyword funnel optional
  • Live within ten days of the shoot

Option B · mobile to desktop

  • 3 Reels: Niseko powder, Sapporo at night, Tokyo in the rain, each edited in Lightroom mobile on location
  • 1 YouTube long-form: the Japan trip from phone to laptop, Lightroom and Photoshop on screen, cut in Premiere Pro
  • Three before-and-after carousels, Justin's Japan preset, and raw clips for Adobe's own cuts
  • 12 months usage across Adobe and Lightroom channels, organic and paid

Option C · the year

  • Everything in Option B
  • New Zealand, the Rockies and Colorado this winter; Costa Rica, Belize, Guatemala and Iceland by March
  • One long-form episode per country, with After Effects title and reveal templates Adobe can reuse
  • A free Lightroom preset pack from the series, released through Adobe to drive installs
Why this works for Adobe The audience that follows Justin for the places is the same audience that asks, in every comment section, how the photo was edited. A series that answers that question with the apps open on screen turns a travel Reel into a product demo without changing what the viewer came for, and it covers the whole suite rather than one tool. His last gear Reel sent 2,074 people to a product link through a DM keyword, and three quarters of them clicked through.
